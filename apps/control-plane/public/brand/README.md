# Zuvel brand assets

The PNG wordmark and symbol are the original transparent images embedded in
pages 6 and 7 of `manual-de-marca-zuvel.pdf` (version 1.0, August 2026), provided
by the project owner. They were extracted without recoloring or distortion.
Keep the official wordmark at least 120 px wide and do not apply glow or shadows
to the logo. The UI uses the manual's violet accent for interactive controls.
